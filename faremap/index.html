South African minibus taxi information for passengers

FareMap is a passenger-focused web experience for browsing minibus taxi routes, reported fares and pickup-point information in South Africa. Information can be incorrect, disputed or outdated; always confirm your destination, actual boarding point, fare and surroundings before travelling. Data-confidence indicators describe the reliability of information and never guarantee physical safety.

FareMap is developed by Spirit2k5 Web Studio in Soweto, Johannesburg. If a fare or pickup location is incorrect, passenger reports can help the project identify information that needs review.

Questions passengers often ask

Are taxi fares guaranteed? No. Fare records are indicative and may differ by taxi association, direction and date. Does FareMap verify location safety? No. Pin accuracy and personal safety are separate questions. Can drivers use this website? Anyone with an existing FareMap account can access passenger features, but the website does not offer Driver Mode.
