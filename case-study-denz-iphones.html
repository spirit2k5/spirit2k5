Case study / E-commerce website

Updated 28 October 2026 · By Carlton Mahlori Tshianeo

Denz iPhones online store.

The Denz iPhones website gives shoppers a way to browse phones by category, compare product choices and move through a cart and checkout journey, with direct support from the Soweto store.

Visit live website ↗Back to work

The project

Make phone choices easier to understand.

The current live store organises phones into Sealed Box, Brand New, Pre-Owned and Cheaper Options. Shoppers can browse the catalogue, compare the information shown for a phone, select available colour and storage options, and add a configuration to their cart. The store asks customers to confirm live stock before payment.

Product clarity

Compare the phone categories

Condition categories and product details help shoppers understand the available options before ordering.

Responsive

Easy to use on a phone

The catalogue and product choices are presented for shoppers using phones as well as larger screens.

Shopping journey

Move from product to checkout

The store includes cart and checkout pages alongside WhatsApp help for stock, product questions and lay-by enquiries.

What you can inspect

A catalogue, product choices and a route to ordering.

Phone categories

Sealed Box, Brand New, Pre-Owned and Cheaper Options provide separate starting points for different shopping needs.

Product choices

Phone pages show the available colour, storage and condition information for the selected product.

Cart and checkout

Customers can build an order through the cart and checkout journey. The site identifies Paystack as its payment provider.

Direct customer support

WhatsApp and contact links provide a route for stock checks, shopping questions, delivery and collection enquiries.

Store and policy information

Visitors can find the Meadowlands store location, delivery and lay-by information, and links to terms, returns and privacy pages.

Live domain

The finished website is live at denziphone.com so potential clients can check the real result for themselves.

Current project version

The live project is an online store.

The live storefront brings the catalogue, product choices and order journey together with customer support and store information. Explore the pages yourself to see how the customer moves from comparing phones to preparing an order.

What you can check
