Privacy Policy

Spirit2k5 Web Studio respects the personal information you share when you contact the studio or request website services.

Information collected

When you send an enquiry, the site may collect your name, business or brand name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, website address, project type, budget range, timeline and the project details you choose to provide.

Why the information is used

The information is used to understand your enquiry, prepare or discuss a project, respond to you, provide requested services and keep reasonable records related to the work.

Form delivery

The project enquiry form may use FormSubmit as a third-party form delivery service so the details can be delivered to Spirit2k5 by email. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for up to 30 days. If you prefer not to use the form, you can contact Spirit2k5 by phone, WhatsApp or direct email.

Sharing and selling

Spirit2k5 does not sell enquiry information to advertisers. Information may be shared only where needed to provide a requested service, comply with law, or use an agreed third-party service such as hosting, a domain provider or a project platform.

Cookies and analytics

The current Spirit2k5 website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies by default. See the Cookie Notice for more detail.

Your choices

You may ask what contact information Spirit2k5 holds about your enquiry, request a correction, or ask for information that is no longer needed to be deleted where applicable.
