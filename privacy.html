Privacy & POPIA Policy

This policy explains how Spirit2k5 Web Studio handles personal information when you browse the website, request a free website check, send an enquiry, communicate about a project or become a client.

Who is responsible for your information

Spirit2k5 Web Studio is operated by Carlton Mahlori Tshianeo. Contact: mahloricarlton@gmail.com, 078 188 8220. Public business address: 29 Thupa, Meadowlands, Soweto, 1853, South Africa.

Information you may provide

This can include your name, business or brand name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, website address, budget range, timeline, project requirements, files or content you choose to share, and messages related to your enquiry or project.

Why the information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare or discuss quotes, provide the requested website service, communicate about revisions and launch, provide support, prevent abuse of forms, and keep reasonable business records connected to the work.

POPIA approach

Spirit2k5 aims to process personal information only for a specific and legitimate business purpose, keep it reasonably accurate, protect it against unauthorised access, and retain it only for as long as it is reasonably needed or legally required.

Project enquiry forms

The project enquiry form may use FormSubmit to deliver form information by email. If you prefer not to use the form, you can contact Spirit2k5 directly by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Third-party services

Information may be processed through services used to deliver a project or communicate with you, such as email, WhatsApp, website hosting, domain providers, form delivery, WordPress, payment providers or other project platforms where relevant. Only information reasonably needed for the service should be shared.

Cookies and analytics

The current Spirit2k5 website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies by default. Essential browser or hosting functions may still process technical information needed to load and protect the website. See the Cookie Notice.

Your choices and rights

You may ask what personal information Spirit2k5 holds about you, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing where applicable, or request deletion of information that no longer needs to be kept. Legal and business record-keeping requirements may limit deletion in some cases.

Complaints

If a privacy concern cannot be resolved directly, you may also contact South Africa’s Information Regulator. See inforegulator.org.za.
