Terms of Service

These are the general terms for direct website projects with Spirit2k5 Web Studio. A specific written quote, scope or agreement can add to or replace parts of these terms for a particular project.

Project scope

The pages, features, integrations, content responsibilities and delivery requirements are agreed before or during the build. New features or major changes outside the agreed scope may require a revised quote.

Payment process

For normal direct website projects, Spirit2k5 builds the agreed work first so the client can see and review it before the website-design payment is due. After approval and payment, the approved website can be deployed or connected to the agreed domain and hosting.

Third-party costs

Domains, hosting, paid plugins, payment-provider charges and other third-party services are separate unless specifically included in the quote. These costs are confirmed before Spirit2k5 buys or activates them for a client.

Client content

Clients are responsible for having the right to use the logos, photos, text, trademarks and other material they provide for a project.

Revisions

Reasonable revisions to the agreed work can be handled during the review stage. Requests that materially change the original scope may be treated as additional work.

Launch and support

Launch arrangements and post-launch support depend on the selected package or agreed project scope. Where a package states a support period, that period applies to reasonable fixes related to the delivered work.

SEO and business results

Spirit2k5 can build search-friendly technical foundations, but no specific Google ranking, traffic level, sales figure or enquiry volume is guaranteed.
