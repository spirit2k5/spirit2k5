Selling online

A store designed around the way your customers shop.

The e-commerce package is for businesses that need more than a product list. Products can be organised into clear categories with useful information, options and pricing, while the cart and checkout flow are kept simple on mobile and desktop. Depending on the project, the store can also include delivery or collection choices, customer details, payment-provider integration and stock presentation so customers know what is available before ordering. The layout is also planned to keep product information, trust details and contact options easy to find throughout the shopping journey.

Before your store is built

Plan the catalogue, orders and customer information.

Products and options

Prepare product names, descriptions, prices and images you have permission to use. List options such as size, colour or storage, and explain how stock or availability should appear. The number of product entries and variations belongs in the agreed scope.

Payments and order handling

Choose how customers will pay and how your business will receive and manage orders. Online payment integration depends on the selected provider, its account approval and the project requirements. Provider fees and account conditions still apply.

Delivery, collection and policies

Decide where you deliver, how charges are calculated and whether collection is available. Supply the business contact details and applicable delivery, returns and privacy information customers need before placing an order.

Launch checks

The agreed shopping journey is checked from product selection through cart and checkout, including mobile use. Payment-provider setup, order notifications and delivery rules need the correct business details and account access before launch.

Common questions

Online store questions.

Does the starting price cover every product and integration?

The E-commerce Website package starts from R6,500. Product quantities, variations, payment integrations and delivery requirements are confirmed in the quote. The starting price does not include unlimited product entry or every possible integration.

Can customers pay online or arrange collection?

Those options can be included when agreed in the scope. Online payments depend on a suitable approved provider account; delivery or collection choices depend on the rules your business supplies.

When do I pay, and what support follows launch?

You review and approve the agreed store before website-design payment is due. The approved store is then launched. The package includes 30 days of support for reasonable fixes after launch; third-party services and later additions remain subject to the quote.

How payment works

See your store before you pay.

You see and review the agreed store before the website-design payment is due. Once you are happy, you pay. After payment, the approved store is launched and the domain or hosting is connected. Any third-party cost is confirmed with you before it is purchased or activated.
