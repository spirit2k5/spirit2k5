What changes the price?

The scope matters more than the label.

Number of pages

A focused three-page site takes less planning and content work than a larger site with separate service, portfolio and information pages.

Custom features

Bookings, customer accounts, dashboards, advanced forms and live information require more work than a normal business website.

E-commerce requirements

Products, variations, stock, delivery or collection options, payment setup and checkout logic can change the amount of work in an online store.

Content and media

The project is easier when final text and good-quality images are ready. If content needs restructuring or many assets need preparation, that adds work.

Third-party services

Domains, hosting plans, paid plugins, payment services or other external tools are separate third-party costs when they are required.

Existing website condition

A redesign may be straightforward if the current content is usable, or more involved if the structure, mobile layout and functionality all need rebuilding.

Payment process

For normal website projects, you see the work first.

For normal direct website projects, you review the agreed work before the website-design payment is due. After payment, the approved website is launched and the approved domain or hosting is connected. Any third-party cost is explained before it is purchased or activated.
