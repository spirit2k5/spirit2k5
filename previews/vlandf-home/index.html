Lawrence Vonani Mhlongo is the founder of VL & F IT SERVICES which was initially registered as VLAND F GENERAL TRADIG AND PROJECTS (PTY) LTD, He was born at Xihoko Village in Tzaneen town of Limpopo province in South Africa. He was introduced to IT since year 2003 when he studied MCSE 2003, N+, A+ in Advanced Concepts in Pretoria then Cisco CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate) and CCNP (Routing and Switching).Lawrence is internationally certified with valid Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP Routing and Switching) and Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA Routing and Switching).

Lawrence Vonani Mhlongo has acquired hands on experience in ICT from 2006 till to date, he provided IT services to different organizations in the country were he worked for public and private sectors, In year 2006 he worked as a Network Engineer at Masana Technologies in Plan, design, implement, monitor and troubleshoot City of Johannesburg wireless Lan network until 2008 and Dimension Data in 2009 as a Network Engineer/Administrator in Plan, design, implement, monitor and troubleshoot City of Johannesburg WAN and LAN network.
