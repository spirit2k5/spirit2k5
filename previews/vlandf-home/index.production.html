The Founder Profile — Lawrence Vonani Mhlongo

Lawrence Vonani Mhlongo is the founder of VL & F IT SERVICES which was initially registered as VLAND F GENERAL TRADING AND PROJECTS (PTY) LTD, He was born at Xihoko Village in Tzaneen town of Limpopo province in South Africa. He was introduced to IT in 2003 when he studied MCSE 2003, N+, A+ in Advanced Concepts in Pretoria then Cisco CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate) and CCNP (Routing and Switching). Lawrence is internationally certified with valid Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP Routing and Switching) and Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA Routing and Switching).

Lawrence Vonani Mhlongo has acquired hands-on experience in ICT from 2006 to date, he provided IT services to different organisations in the country where he worked for public and private sectors, In 2006 he worked as a Network Engineer at Masana Technologies in Plan, design, implement, monitor and troubleshoot City of Johannesburg wireless LAN network until 2008 and Dimension Data in 2009 as a Network Engineer/Administrator in Plan, design, implement, monitor and troubleshoot City of Johannesburg WAN and LAN network. Routers, wireless devices and switch configurations, remote and site support. Network management and monitoring. Maintaining SLAs and performing standby until 2011 and EOH Managed Services in Plan, design, implement, monitor and troubleshoot City of Johannesburg WAN and LAN network. Routers, wireless devices and switch configurations, remote and site support. Network management and monitoring. Maintaining SLAs and performing standby until 2017.

Lawrence is currently full-time Executive Director of VL&F IT SERVICES in charge of daily technical operation at Institutions such as CETA (Construction Education & Training Authority), SB Power House, Zion Tech Group, Best Value Foods, Lammbtech PTY LTD, Laphinda Security Services, Ridgevale Primary School, Department of Public Works, TMS (Timbavati Medical Services) and Dr Mkhawana Medical Centre.
