Choose the work you actually need

A new WordPress site, a store or help with an existing one.

New business website

Start with the services, pages and contact actions customers need. Decide which information your team should edit regularly. The page count, content work and editing requirements are recorded in the project scope.

WooCommerce store

Plan products, variations, stock presentation and delivery or collection rules before choosing extra plugins. Payment integration depends on the chosen provider and account approval. A store needs a defined order journey as well as product pages.

Existing website and migration

Share the website link and explain what needs to change. Access, hosting, current plugins and a suitable backup or rollback option are checked before agreeing on repairs, a redesign or a move. The existing site determines what can safely be reused.

Editing and upkeep

Plan who will manage the website after launch.

Confirm editing access, handover guidance and who will maintain WordPress, themes and plugins. Hosting and paid licences have their own renewal costs unless your quote includes them. Compare the business website package, e-commerce store requirements and website maintenance options to match the work to your needs.

Common questions

WordPress and WooCommerce questions.

Can I update the website myself?

Tell us which content you want to edit. Editing access and any handover guidance are agreed in the project scope so the setup fits the people maintaining it.

Can you improve my current WordPress website?

Yes, subject to checking the current site and available access. The work may be a focused fix, content update, redesign or migration. The required changes are agreed before work starts.

Are premium plugins and hosting included?

Paid themes, plugins, hosting and other third-party charges are separate unless the written quote includes them. Their provider terms and renewal charges still apply.

Do I pay before reviewing the WordPress website?

For normal direct website projects, you review and approve the agreed work before website-design payment is due. After payment, the approved website is launched. Revisions and timing follow the agreed scope.
