Plan the right pages

What should your business website help customers do?

Pages and customer actions

Start with the action that matters: a call, an enquiry or a quote request. Map the agreed pages around it, such as Home, Services, About and Contact. A gallery or separate service page belongs in the scope when customers need that detail.

Business information and images

Prepare your service descriptions, contact details, logo and images you have permission to use. Identify the information customers ask for repeatedly so the website can answer those questions clearly.

Search and enquiry foundations

Page titles, descriptions and clear headings help explain each page. Contact links and agreed forms are checked on mobile and desktop. Search setup supports discovery, but does not guarantee a particular Google position.

Scope and access

Confirm the page count, required features, content preparation and handover needs in the quote. An existing domain, hosting account or website may need access before changes or launch can be arranged.

Common questions

Business website questions.

How many pages are included?

The Business Website package starts from R3,500 and includes up to six pages. Additional pages, custom features and content work are agreed in the quote rather than assumed to be included.

Are domain and hosting charges included?

Domains, hosting and other third-party services are separate costs unless the written quote includes them. Any such cost is confirmed before it is purchased or activated.

What happens after I approve the website?

After you review and approve the agreed work, website-design payment follows and the approved website is launched. The Business package includes 30 days of support for reasonable fixes after launch; later additions can be quoted separately.

How payment works

See your website before you pay.

You see and review the agreed website before the website-design payment is due. Once you are happy, you pay. After payment, the approved website is launched and the domain or hosting is connected. Any third-party cost is confirmed with you before it is purchased or activated.
