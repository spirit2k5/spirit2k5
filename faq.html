Frequently asked questions

Website design FAQ for Johannesburg & Soweto.

Short answers to the questions businesses usually need to know before asking for a quote.

Start a project →

Do I pay upfront?

For normal direct website projects, no upfront website-design payment is required. You see and review the agreed work before paying.

How long will my website take?

The timeline depends on pages, features, content and how quickly feedback is provided. A realistic timeline is confirmed after the scope is understood.

Can you build WordPress?

Yes. WordPress business websites, WooCommerce stores, redesigns and fixes are available.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Yes. Useful content can be kept while the layout, mobile experience, navigation and outdated sections are improved.

Are domain and hosting included?

Third-party domain, hosting and paid-service costs are confirmed separately unless your quote specifically includes them.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Reasonable revisions to the agreed work are handled during review. Major new features outside the original scope may need a revised quote.

Will I rank #1 on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific Google ranking. Spirit2k5 can set up strong search-friendly website foundations and help with indexing and local-search basics.

Do you work only in Soweto?

No. Spirit2k5 is based in Soweto, Johannesburg and can work with businesses elsewhere in Gauteng and South Africa.

What happens after launch?

Support depends on your package or scope. Business and e-commerce packages currently include 30 days of support for reasonable fixes.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is available on 078 188 8220, and there is a WhatsApp button on every page of this site.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed for once-off updates, fixes or ongoing support. See website maintenance →
