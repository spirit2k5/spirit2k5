Ad hoc or ongoing

Maintenance does not have to mean a long contract.

Some businesses only need help when something changes or breaks. Others need regular updates because their prices, products, services or content change often. I can quote a once-off maintenance task, or we can agree on a recurring support scope if ongoing work actually makes sense for the site.

Before I touch the site

Access and backups matter.

Platform access

I need the correct website, WordPress, hosting or repository access for the work being requested.

Current backup

For higher-risk changes, a recent backup or a safe rollback option should be available before major edits are made.

Scope first

If the website has many underlying problems, I will explain whether a focused fix or a wider redesign makes more sense before starting.

Third-party services

Hosting, paid plugins or external services remain subject to their own plans, charges and technical limits.

Questions

Website maintenance FAQ.

Can you maintain a website you did not build?

Often yes, but I review the platform and access first before agreeing to changes.

Do I need monthly maintenance?

No. Ongoing maintenance is optional. You can ask for once-off fixes when needed.

Can maintenance include SEO fixes?

Technical or on-page SEO fixes can be included when they are part of the agreed maintenance work.

How much does maintenance cost?

It depends on the work required and whether the support is once-off or recurring. I quote after seeing the current site and the changes needed.
