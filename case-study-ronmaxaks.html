Case study / Business website

RONMAXAKS Security Solutions.

A security-services business needed a professional online presence that could explain what the company does, make the services easier to understand and give customers a clear route to contact the business.

Visit live website ↗Back to work

The project

A business site has to make trust easier.

For a service company, the website is often one of the first places a potential customer checks before making contact. The job was therefore not only to make the site look modern. The information needed a clear structure, the company needed to feel legitimate, and important actions such as viewing services or getting in touch needed to be obvious on both desktop and mobile.

Business UX

Clear service structure

The site separates business information into understandable sections so visitors can quickly work out what RONMAXAKS offers.

Responsive

Works across screen sizes

Layouts and navigation were built to remain usable on phones as well as larger desktop screens.

Live

Public result

The finished website is available on its own domain and can be checked directly rather than treated as a concept-only portfolio piece.

What I handled

The website from structure to launch.

Page structure

Organised the content around the information a potential customer needs first: who the business is, what it offers and how to get in touch.

Responsive interface

Built the front-end layout so the site stays readable and usable across common device sizes.

Service presentation

Structured the business services so visitors do not have to search through dense text to understand the offer.

Contact flow

Made the next step clear for visitors who want to enquire or contact the security business.

Testing and refinement

Checked the interface during development and adjusted the site before launch instead of treating the first draft as finished.

Domain launch

The project moved from development into a public live website on ronmaxaks.com.
