Cookie & Browser Storage Notice

Spirit2k5 keeps website tracking deliberately limited. The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers.

Temporary session storage

The website may use your browser's sessionStorage to create a temporary random session identifier. This helps group first-party page and conversion events during a browsing session without creating an advertising profile. Session storage is normally cleared by the browser when the session ends.

First-party measurement

Limited interaction events can be sent to Spirit2k5's own Supabase-backed event store. These can include page views, Start Project clicks, WhatsApp clicks, pricing and portfolio clicks, free website check clicks, project-form progress, AI assistant opens or response-layer events, and enquiry outcomes. Conversion analytics do not store the text of an AI question. The record can include page path, referrer, campaign parameters and browser user-agent information.

Third-party services

Some features open or use third-party services, such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, FormSubmit or embedded/live client links. If you choose to use those services, they may apply their own cookies or browser-storage rules under their own privacy policies.

Future analytics tools

If Spirit2k5 later enables a service such as Google Analytics or another tool that materially changes cookie or tracking behaviour, this notice and the Privacy & POPIA Policy should be updated to reflect that setup.

Your browser controls

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. Blocking browser storage may affect some website functionality or measurement but should not prevent you from contacting Spirit2k5 directly by email, phone or WhatsApp.
