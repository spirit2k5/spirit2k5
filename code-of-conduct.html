The principles we work by

What you can expect from Spirit2k5.

These principles guide the way we quote, design, communicate and support website projects.

01

Honesty & transparency

We explain what is included, what is not included and what a project will cost. We do not knowingly mislead clients about features, progress, experience or results.

02

Respect & fairness

We treat clients and collaborators with courtesy, listen to feedback and provide fair service without unfair discrimination or unnecessary pressure.

03

Accountability & communication

We take responsibility for our agreed work, communicate material changes or delays and work to resolve mistakes or concerns professionally.

04

Privacy & confidentiality

We handle client information with care, request access only when needed and avoid sharing private project details without a proper reason or permission. Read our Privacy & POPIA Policy for more.

05

Quality & care

We aim to deliver usable, responsive websites that match the agreed brief, check important features before launch and address issues within the agreed scope.

06

Responsible marketing & conduct

We present services and examples truthfully, respect client-owned content and intellectual property, and do not promise guaranteed Google rankings, traffic or sales.

Fair dealing

How this works on a real project.

A clear process helps both sides know what to expect before making a decision.

01

Clear scope and quote

We discuss your goals, the planned pages or features, expected costs and any important dependencies before work proceeds.

02

Review the work before design payment

For normal direct website projects, you can see and review the agreed website work before the website-design payment becomes due. Different arrangements, if any, must be agreed clearly for the specific project.

03

Approve extra costs first

Domain registration, hosting, paid plugins and other third-party services are separate unless included in your written quote. We discuss purchases we are asked to make on your behalf before making them.

04

Feedback, approval and launch

You can raise concerns during review. We assess requested changes against the agreed scope and launch or transfer the approved work under the agreed project terms.

This Code of Conduct explains our service standards. It does not replace the Terms of Service, Cancellations & Refunds policy or a specific written project agreement. Applicable consumer rights remain in place.
