Common questions

Johannesburg web design FAQ.

How much does a website cost in Johannesburg?

Starter websites begin from R1,500, business websites from R3,500 and e-commerce websites from R6,500. Custom systems are quoted according to scope.

Do I need to pay upfront?

For normal Spirit2k5 website projects, you can review the agreed work before the website-design payment is due.

Do you build WordPress websites?

Yes. WordPress and WooCommerce websites can be created, redesigned or fixed depending on what your business needs.

Do you serve businesses outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Spirit2k5 is based in Soweto and can work with businesses across Gauteng and the rest of South Africa.
