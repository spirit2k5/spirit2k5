Why these guides exist

Good website decisions start before money is spent.

If you are not sure whether your business needs a simple site, WordPress, e-commerce, a redesign or something custom, these guides help you compare the options before you commit. The goal is to help you understand what your business genuinely needs, what can wait until later and what should be fixed first.

Which path fits?

New site, redesign, WordPress or custom?

Start fresh

If the business has no useful website yet, a focused Starter or Business Website is usually the cleanest route.

Redesign

If the domain and content are still useful but the experience feels old or difficult on mobile, redesign may make more sense than starting from zero.

WordPress

WordPress is useful when the business wants editable pages, regular content changes, plugins or WooCommerce.

Custom online solution

A custom online solution is better suited to businesses that need customer accounts, dashboards, bookings, live information or workflows beyond a normal business website.
