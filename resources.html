Why these guides exist

Good website decisions start before the build.

A lot of small businesses are not sure whether they need a simple site, WordPress, e-commerce, a redesign or something custom. These resources are meant to make those choices clearer before money is spent. I explain what affects price, what a redesign should actually fix, what local businesses should look for in a website and which questions are worth asking a developer before a project starts. The goal is not to push every visitor into the biggest package. It is to help you understand what your business genuinely needs, what can wait until later and what should be fixed first.

Which path fits?

New site, redesign, WordPress or custom?

Start fresh

If the business has no useful website yet, a focused Starter or Business Website is usually the cleanest route.

Redesign

If the domain and content are still useful but the experience feels old or difficult on mobile, redesign may make more sense than starting from zero.

WordPress

WordPress is useful when the business wants editable pages, regular content changes, plugins or WooCommerce.

Custom build

A custom web app is better suited to workflows that need accounts, dashboards, databases, APIs or behaviour beyond a normal marketing website.
