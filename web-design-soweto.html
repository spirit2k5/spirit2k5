Common questions

Soweto web design FAQ.

How much does web design cost in Soweto?

Starter websites begin from R1,500, business websites from R3,500 and e-commerce websites from R6,500. Custom systems are quoted according to scope.

Do I have to pay before seeing my website?

No. For normal Spirit2k5 website projects, you can review the agreed work before the website-design payment is due.

Can you add WhatsApp and enquiry forms?

Yes. Websites can include WhatsApp links, click-to-call actions, enquiry forms and other contact options depending on the project.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. Online stores can include products, categories, carts, checkout and the features your business needs to sell online.
